Our Origin

Four guys.
One set.
A shared vision.

Adam, Braden, Jai, and David met on set as extras in the movie Chasing Summer in St. Louis. They loved the experience and decided they wanted to create an opportunity to take the lead in a feature film.

So they started writing, networking, and building ABCM Media — a production company rooted in story, collaboration, and the belief that great work can be built right here in St. Louis.

Our Mission

Build here. Create here. Grow here.

ABCM's goal is simple: build a production company in St. Louis that creates high-quality content while investing back into the local creative community.
