Our Origin

Four guys.One set.A shared vision.

Adam Brewer, Braden Clark, Jai Thornhill, and David Dobkowski Jr. met while working as extras on Chasing Summer, a feature filmed in St. Louis.

They loved the experience and decided they wanted to create an opportunity to take the lead in a feature film of their own.

They began writing, networking, building relationships, and learning how to turn that idea into something real. ABCM Media grew from that shared vision.
